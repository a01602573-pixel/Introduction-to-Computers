Computers are the *electronic devices* that takes the input in the form of *raw data* and process it into meaningful output.<br>

## 2.Types of Computers:<br>
Computers are being classified on the basis of there size,power and functions etc.<br>
1.***Super Computers:***<br>
These are the largest and fastest computers that processes massive amount of data for complex tasks.<br>
2.***Mainframe Computers:***<br>
Large,powerful systems used by big organizations.They handle high-volume data processing and transactions etc.<br>
3.***Mini Computers:***<br>
Medium sized computers that comes between mainframes and microcomputers.They support industrial transactions etc.<br>
4.***Micro Computers:***<br>
Small,affordable computers designed for single users.This includes laptops,tablets etc.<br>

## 3.History of computers:<br>
The evolution of computers is generally divided into *five distinct generations.*<br>

| Generation | Period | Core Technology|
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1st Generation** | 1940-1956 | Vacuum Tubes |
| **2nd Generation** | 1956-1963 | Transistors |
| **3rd Generation** | 1963-1971 | Integrated Circuits |
| **4th Generation** | 1971-present | Microprocessors |
| **5th Generation** | present-onwards | Artificial Intelligence |
